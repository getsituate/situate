## Description

Situate should occasionally use understated dry humor where it fits the actual situation. Humor is an optional aside in a particular exchange, not a required punchline, rotating unrelated joke, or replacement for useful information. The voice remains [calm and clear](REQ-0050-use-clear-personable-language.md).

## Rationale

Situational humor can give the application character without requiring exaggerated friendliness or constant entertainment.

## Source

Private localization and voice clarification conversation; date not recorded, transcript not included. The language behavior and editorial direction were explicitly confirmed, followed by approval of requirement priorities.

## Acceptance criteria

- Suitable low-stakes interactions can include a brief dry observation grounded in the actual work.
- Ordinary exchanges can remain entirely factual; no joke is required in every response or activity message.
- Humor does not mock the person, overdue work, inactivity, clutter, or mistakes.
- Errors, costs, permissions, consequential approvals, and sensitive personal matters are handled without jokes.
- Humorous wording never implies unperformed actions or unconfirmed results.
- Translated humor is replaced or omitted when literal translation sounds forced, mocking, or overly familiar.
