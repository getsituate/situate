# Hierarchy and rhythm review

Identify the task and intended first, next and supporting information. Inspect the requested rendered surface and relevant styles; do not infer visual quality from source alone.

Check whether emphasis matches task importance through placement, type, contrast and spacing. Examine grouping, alignment, readable line lengths and consistency across comparable elements. Test realistic long and sparse content and relevant container sizes.

Use existing scales and components where they serve the task. Do not impose arbitrary pixel grids, color counts, saturated primary buttons or novelty. Intentional differences are not automatically inconsistencies.

Separate confirmed readability or action-discovery problems from style preferences. Recommend narrow changes with reasons. In review-only work, leave files untouched. When edits are authorized, preserve behavior and recheck the rendered result and accessibility.

Report the few highest-impact findings, their evidence, any changes made and remaining uncertainty. Do not claim a successful user comprehension test from an expert's visual inspection.
