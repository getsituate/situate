=== Requirements overview

Situate is a personal organizational system for one person.
Its core provides task, note, knowledge and document management.
Situate connects these capabilities with external email, calendar and supplementary data sources.
Tasks and notes managed by Situate are brought into its core; document originals may remain in connected sources.
Situate uses AI for routine and preparatory work, drawing on personal context and the user's working methods.
AI supports personal organization rather than defining the product category.

People who consciously organize their personal lives currently have to maintain connections between information, tasks and commitments across multiple tools.
Situate should reduce this maintenance and the effort of handling individual processes, while preserving the user's preferred organizational method.
Technical expertise is not a prerequisite.
The link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/foundations.md[foundations] define the audience, internal core, external services and single-person boundary.

An <<glossary-organizational-process,organizational process>> is an ongoing matter that may involve information and next steps across tasks, notes, documents and external services.
For example, a request for missing documents may require finding existing material, recording work needed to prepare the remainder, reserving time in a connected calendar and preparing a reply.
Situate should retain the relationship between these steps when the remaining material becomes available, rather than treating each interaction as unrelated.
This is an illustrative intended workflow, not evidence of implemented functionality.

The <<glossary>> defines shared product and architecture terms. The catalog defines the desired outcomes:

|===
|Goal |Intended outcome
|link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0001-connected-organization.md[GOAL-0001]
|Keep personal organization connected without continual manual maintenance.
|link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0002-organizational-processes.md[GOAL-0002]
|Handle organizational processes with less personal time and mental effort.
|link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0003-track-commitments.md[GOAL-0003]
|Keep commitments and follow-up work visible without remembering them separately.
|link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0004-decision-authority.md[GOAL-0004]
|Delegate internal and external actions within the user's request, without per-action approval. Internal changes remain reversible; users can inspect outcomes and stop ongoing work.
|link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0005-personal-connection.md[GOAL-0005]
|Experience the system as personally fitting and supportive.
|===

Relevant capabilities include link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0004-use-existing-context.md[using existing context], link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0005-record-process-history.md[maintaining process history], link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0001-create-integrations.md[user-requested integrations] and link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0013-synchronization-service.md[synchronization across the user's devices].
The catalog also specifies link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0034-adapt-task-workflows.md[adaptable task workflows], link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0036-convert-imported-notes-and-tasks.md[import conversion] and link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0054-connect-document-sources.md[connected document sources], with link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0055-search-document-content.md[content search] and a link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0058-browse-document-collection.md[common document view].
Their open questions include initial task filters, reconciliation after repeated imports and shared connector contracts.

The link:{architecture-docs-root}/product-concept.md[product concept] describes the free local application and optional paid synchronization and managed AI services.
These are intended capabilities and business direction, not a description of a shipped system.
The repository contains a client implementation; <<current-client-packages>> describes its package responsibilities.
That implementation does not establish complete product delivery or native platform qualification.
Reported benefits are based on individual experience; broader demand and the extent of the benefit remain to be validated.
