=== Stakeholders

[cols="1,3",options="header"]
|===
|Role |Concern
|Individual user
|Delegate routine organizational work, preserve personal context and working methods, access content across devices, and control actions taken toward others.
|Situate product and service operator
|Provide a useful local application and optional sustainable services without making access to stored content depend on purchasing those services. Pricing and service commitments remain open.
|Developer and operator
|Keep delivery and operational complexity within one person's capacity, as required by the link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/foundations.md#development-operation-and-technical-baseline[development and operation constraint].
|===

Other people may receive communications requested by the user or collaborate in connected external services.
They are not co-users of a shared Situate workspace; these interactions belong to xref:context-scope[Context and Scope].
