=== Product and organizational constraints

[cols="1,2,2",options="header"]
|===
|Constraint and source |Scope |Architectural consequence
|Single developer and operator; link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/foundations.md#development-operation-and-technical-baseline[foundations]
|Development, delivery and operation are handled by one person.
|Implementation and operational complexity must fit that capacity. Four-digit monthly costs before revenue are conceivable, but no exact ceiling or spending authorization is established.
|New technical baseline; link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/foundations.md#development-operation-and-technical-baseline[foundations]
|No implementation from the previous prototype is carried forward.
|There is no prototype technology or compatibility mandate. Accepted technology choices are recorded in <<solution-strategy>>.
|Personal organizational core; link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/foundations.md#personal-organizational-core[foundations]
|Tasks, knowledge and documents belong to Situate's core. Email and calendar remain external. Situate does not provide built-in workspaces shared by multiple users.
|Built-in device synchronization serves one person, not collaborative editing between users. Custom plugins do not imply built-in collaboration guarantees. External integrations supplement rather than replace the core.
|Optional paid services; link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0015-connect-user-provided-models.md[REQ-0015]
|Local use with supported user-provided model access does not require paid Situate services.
|Neither synchronization subscriptions nor the managed AI router may be mandatory dependencies for that use.
|Personal working methods; link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/foundations.md#personal-working-methods[foundations]
|No single organizational method is mandatory.
|The content model and workflows must not require users to adopt a method such as Getting Things Done (GTD).
|===
