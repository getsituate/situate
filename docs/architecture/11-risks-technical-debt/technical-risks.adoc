=== Technical risks

These entries describe risks in the documented design, not demonstrated implementation defects or proof of verified mitigation.
The CTO owns technical assessment and verification for every entry; marketplace terms and operating commitments also require product and legal decisions.
Marketplace review/report operations still need an agreed operational owner before delivery.

Impact judgments describe potential consequences: high means possible loss of data or confidentiality, incompatible execution, or application-wide unavailability; medium means constrained delivery or degraded operation.
Likelihood is unknown where occurrence rates and operating evidence are unavailable; the entries do not estimate probabilities.
An accepted trade-off remains a risk, and a documented mechanism does not close its verification questions.

[[plugin-development-risks]]
==== Plugin development and adoption

===== Source and active code can diverge

Basis: link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0033-stage-and-activate-plugin-changes.md[REQ-0033] and <<runtime-view>>.

Reading editable source as the execution version could load unchecked code or combine incompatible plugin revisions.
The runtime description separates editable source from immutable Git revisions.
It records selected active revisions in the <<glossary-active-revision-map,Active Revision Map>>, replacing affected selections together during plugin activation publication.
A <<glossary-check-receipt,Check Receipt>> records which revisions and checks were covered; it does not itself activate code.
A durable plugin activation attempt distinguishes confirmed activation from an unresolved outcome.

Assessment: high impact; likelihood unknown. Status: mechanisms documented; interrupted-switch and multi-plugin consistency verification remain open here.

Verification and review trigger: when activation storage or switching changes, check interrupted activation, retained history and consistent loading of related plugins.
Preserve known outcomes: a failed refresh or notification is not proof that activation failed and does not authorize blind repetition.

===== Checks may cover outdated dependency revisions

Basis: link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0033-stage-and-activate-plugin-changes.md[REQ-0033].

Concurrent developments can change dependencies or consumers after checks.
A Check Receipt for earlier revisions does not establish compatibility with the activation state; activating unselected plugins could overwrite independent work.
Source or dependency changes require fresh applicable checks before activation.
Revision-bound checks and coordinated activation are documented, but they do not promise conflict-free concurrent development.
Overlapping development may interfere with or overwrite changes without a development lock, base-version rejection, mandatory reconciliation or automatic merge.

Assessment: high impact; likelihood unknown. Status: concurrent interference accepted; verification of dependency drift remains open here.

Verification and review trigger: when dependency selection or activation coordination changes, check concurrent edits, stale receipts and long-running affected actions.
Activation must wait for those actions without aborting or repeating them.
Check failed-switch recovery within these constraints rather than introducing a competing-development lock or base-version rejection policy.

===== Trial safety depends on instructions

Basis: link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0033-stage-and-activate-plugin-changes.md[REQ-0033].

Agent-led investigation can read workspace data and connected accounts.
Instructions forbidding incidental real external writes cannot technically prevent a model mistake, prompt injection or trusted plugin bypass.
Source staging does not isolate external accounts.

Assessment: high impact; likelihood unknown. Status: instruction-only boundary accepted; behavioral verification remains necessary.

Verification and review trigger: when development instructions, tools or reusable connections change, verify instruction propagation, task scope and trial behavior across connection calls.
Keep automated tests strictly synthetic.
Any stronger technical restriction needs a separate decision; neither tests nor review imply an enforcement guarantee.

===== Generated artifacts may contain personal material

Basis: link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0033-stage-and-activate-plugin-changes.md[REQ-0033] and link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0019-share-plugins-through-marketplace.md[REQ-0019].

Code, fixtures, screenshots or a marketplace publication copy can accidentally embed personal data or credentials, especially after real-data trials.

Assessment: high impact; likelihood unknown. Status: artifact review required; no guarantee of harmlessness or legal clearance.

Verification and review trigger: check generated artifacts after real-data investigation and review the separate marketplace publication copy before submission.
Use synthetic screenshot data and exercise the complete store-page preview.
Manual approval and local review reduce risk but do not guarantee that private material or third-party rights issues have been excluded.

===== Incomplete developments may accumulate

Basis: link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0033-stage-and-activate-plugin-changes.md[REQ-0033].

Leftover development artifacts may grow without a retention limit; actual sizes and growth rates are unmeasured.
The MVP has no development-storage display or manual cleanup capability.
Closing a view does not stop ongoing work or delete storage.

Assessment: impact unknown until storage growth and disk pressure are measured; likelihood unknown. Status: leftover growth accepted for the MVP without age-based cleanup.

Verification and review trigger: reassess storage policy if measured growth causes disk pressure.
Continue removing disposable check state while preserving live files, active code, configurations, history, conversations and evidence of uncertain plugin activation outcomes.
Workspace closure and application quit must stop development work; reopening interrupted work waits for user interaction.

===== Marketplace review has capacity and protection limits

Basis: link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0019-share-plugins-through-marketplace.md[REQ-0019], link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0020-automate-plugin-security-approval.md[REQ-0020] and link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0021-adapt-local-plugin-copies.md[REQ-0021].

Initial manual per-version review requires operator capacity; automated malware/prompt-injection review is deferred.
Blocking prevents adoption of a marketplace version but neither changes existing independent local copies nor warns their users of later security findings.
Local security warnings and inconclusive results remain explicitly overridable for the exact candidate and relevant dependency revisions; an override does not waive failed functional or compatibility checks.

Assessment: medium impact for review-capacity constraints; high impact for harmful adopted code; likelihood unknown. Status: manual review and independent-copy limits accepted; operating arrangements and publication terms remain open.

Verification and review trigger: before marketplace delivery, agree review/report ownership and operations, and test immediate blocking at adoption, per-version approval and rejection feedback.
Assess uniform reuse terms without promising legal clearance or selecting a project license.
Reassess capacity when submission or report evidence becomes available, and protection limits when security findings arise after adoption.

See <<strategy-plugin-updates>> and <<runtime-view>> for development and activation lifecycle boundaries.

==== Application availability and history recovery

===== A plugin view can freeze the application

Basis: link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/foundations.md#plugin-trust-and-synchronization-ownership[plugin trust and synchronization ownership] and link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0032-manage-plugin-status-and-disabling.md[REQ-0032].

Plugin views may run as ordinary Vue components in the host UI without an independently stoppable rendering context.
A synchronous loop or other view failure can freeze the application, affect other open workspaces and require forced restart, with possible loss of unsaved state.
In-app abort and view removal cannot guarantee stopping such code; service and chat-tool supervision remain unchanged.

Assessment: high impact; likelihood unknown. Status: accepted availability trade-off, conditional on plugin-free startup and repair; platform-specific recovery entry still requires verification.

Verification and review trigger: when plugin loading or recovery entry changes, verify that recovery is reachable after a frozen run and suppresses plugins before loading or execution across restored and subsequently opened workspaces.
Built-in management and agent repair must remain usable without executing suspended plugins.
Inspection or editing alone must not resume them; checked activation resumes its target only.
Recovery is not a sandbox or rollback, and testing or resuming faulty code may freeze the application again.
See <<glossary-recovery-mode,Recovery Mode>> and <<strategy-plugin-recovery-start>>.

===== Uncontrolled internal-file synchronization can corrupt history

Basis: link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/foundations.md#workspace-storage-and-history[workspace storage and history] and link:{architecture-repository-root}/requirements/data/requirements/REQ-0022-explain-note-changes.md#damaged-repository-recovery[REQ-0022: damaged repository recovery].

Uncontrolled synchronization or modification of internal files can make repository history unusable.
This <<glossary-repository-corruption,repository corruption>> is distinct from a merge conflict in an intact repository.
Synchronization plugins supply transport; versioning, conflict handling and history recovery remain core responsibilities for every transport.
A preserved damaged backup does not guarantee recoverable history, and history preservation does not protect against physical damage.

Assessment: high impact; likelihood unknown. Status: recognized risk; the recovery procedure and synchronization protocol boundaries remain open.

Verification and review trigger: before adopting or changing an internal-file transport or damaged-history recovery procedure, verify detection and reporting without asserting an unverified cause.
History reinitialization requires explicit confirmation of prior-history loss and a preserved damaged-repository backup; it must not proceed if that backup cannot be preserved.
Existing content remains the new baseline, and local reinitialization does not itself synchronize or delete histories elsewhere.
See <<glossary-history-reinitialization,History Reinitialization>>.
