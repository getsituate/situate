== Architecture Constraints

The link:../../requirements/data/foundations.md[foundations] and referenced requirements define the constraints below.
They are approved boundaries for design, not claims about implemented safeguards.
The product owner can clarify or change product, budget and distribution boundaries; the CTO clarifies technical restrictions and their consequences within those boundaries.
Legal obligations require assessment against the actual processing and cannot be waived by a product decision.
