# 6. Brand assets and examples

Situate's visual identity is called **Bend in Motion**: warm paper, plum-colored text, clear saturated color, generous curves and Bend, an open violet arch with an expressive face. The overall feel is personal, warm and expressive.

## Let the content do the work

Use quiet, readable surfaces for explanations. Expressive illustration can introduce a topic or mark an occasional meaningful moment. Keep the page understandable without the character or animation.

Choose one focal illustration and keep text outside the artwork. Do not add glossy effects, noisy textures or stock robots to imitate the visual style.

## Color and typography

{{palette}}

Use plum text on paper or the light surface. Deep violet suits text emphasis. Use brighter colors sparingly for accents and emphasis. Keep meaningful text and graphics legible; do not communicate a distinction through color alone.

The brand uses installed system fonts, with Avenir Next where available and system sans-serif fallbacks. Do not download or redistribute font files to match a screenshot. Use the supplied wordmark artwork.

## Wordmark and character

For official Situate communications, follow the guidance below. It does not prohibit adaptations permitted by the artwork license.

Keep the wordmark's proportions and orange dot. Use the dark version on light backgrounds and the light version on plum. Leave clear space of at least half its displayed height; do not skew it, outline it or recolor its letters.

Keep Bend's open silhouette, face and lavender material intact. Scale uniformly; do not mirror the figure, close its opening, crop it into a circular avatar or generate a lookalike. Use the supplied square app icon for square identity placements. Use the dedicated Mini Bend artwork for compact placements.
