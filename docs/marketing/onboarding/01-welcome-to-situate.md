# 1. Welcome to Situate

This pack brings together Situate's product introduction, audience thinking, writing guidance and visual assets for marketing and social media.

## The idea in one paragraph

Situate is a personal organizational system for one person. It brings tasks, knowledge and documents together, connects external services such as email and calendars, and uses AI to carry organizational work forward. People should be able to shape their tools around the way they work. They can describe a tool or view they need and develop it with Situate through conversation.

## Read in this order

1. **Welcome to Situate** introduces the pack.
2. **Situate: a product introduction** explains the experience, everyday scenarios and development stage. Find it in **02-product-introduction**.
3. **Audience, problems and positioning** supports message development and audience research.
4. **Brand voice and writing examples** provides language to adapt for posts and replies.
5. **Content and community playbook** offers content ideas and ways to learn from conversations.
6. **Brand assets and examples** explains which artwork and interface previews to use.

## Using this pack

Use these documents as references when planning content, answering community questions or working with Situate's visual assets. Bring unresolved product questions into the team's discussion before making public claims.

## Product status and examples

The pack describes Situate's intended experience. The product introduction also identifies what the separate Apple Silicon Mac prototype already offers, including conversations, notes and tasks, custom tools and Apple connections. The main application is in development for macOS and iOS; its complete experience is not yet established as available end to end.

Check the current product documentation before presenting a workflow as available. Interface previews use invented content and should carry an illustrative-preview label wherever they are shared.
