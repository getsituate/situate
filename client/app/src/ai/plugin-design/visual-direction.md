# Visual identity

Situate is a personal place for tasks, knowledge and documents. Its identity combines warm paper, plum-colored type, clear accents and generous curves. Daily work is calm and content-led; expressive illustration belongs to occasional moments. Interpret that identity for the person's activity rather than copying a fixed layout.

Use the inherited typography, current host accent and shared components as the foundation. Custom compositions and components remain possible. Bend's geometry comes from the supplied artwork, not a new drawing.

## Host token roles

- --situate-paper and --situate-surface: background and content surfaces.
- --situate-ink and --situate-muted: primary and supporting text.
- --situate-violet-deep and --situate-cobalt: emphasis and focus.
- --situate-line and --situate-control-line: dividers and control boundaries.
- --situate-text-_, --situate-space-_ and --situate-radius-*: typography, spacing and curves.

These are inherited roles, not a palette to redefine per view. Exact control variants are documented in components.
