# Brand principles

[Handbook](../README.md) · [Visual reference](../reference/index.html)

## A personal world with room to work

Situate helps one person organize tasks, knowledge, and documents, connected to external services. Its identity should make a collection feel personally meaningful without making the interface itself the center of attention. A reading list, a household plan, and notes about a hobby belong here as naturally as paid work.

Bend is company in that world, not its owner, a manager, or an agent making independent commitments. The person supplies the purpose. Content, source references, and explicit actions explain what the system does; a character expression cannot replace them.

The visual signature is the combination of five things:

1. **Warm, usable ground:** paper `#f6f3ee` around surface `#fffefa`, with plum ink `#30203e` rather than cold black-on-white chrome.
2. **Distinct color:** violet, sky, cobalt, tangerine, peach, and lemon in deliberate groups, not a continuous rainbow treatment on every control.
3. **Bent, open shapes:** broad arches, looping ribbons, folded paper, and rounded ends. Curves connect objects without enclosing everything in bubbles.
4. **A recognizable companion:** the refined violet open arch, expressive brows and mouth, slim rounded arms, and visible negative space. Use the [character kit](../bend/README.md), not a generic smiley shape.
5. **Contrast in pace:** large close-set display type and abundant illustration at a welcome; compact, readable type and still surfaces while working.

Removing all color, using a tiny mascot badge on a generic dashboard, or replacing the body font with monospace does not preserve this identity.

## Two levels of expression

| Decision           | Brand moment                                                                                            | Daily work                                                                                                                         |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Typical context    | Welcome, explanatory page, a deliberate substantial completion                                          | Reading, editing, finding a note, checking a task, reviewing a result                                                              |
| Page ground        | Paper, with one bounded dark or bright illustration stage                                               | Paper and surface; tinted areas separate context rather than compete                                                               |
| Headline           | 54–94 px desktop hero, often two short lines                                                            | 26–37 px context title; 14–16 px content and actions                                                                               |
| Color distribution | A scene may use three saturated colors plus paper and ink                                               | One main accent; other colors appear in small content-specific objects                                                             |
| Bend               | One scene-sized character, typically 185–352 px                                                         | Usually absent; one static 64–96 px character when useful, or the separate [Mini Bend](../bend/mini/README.md) mark at small sizes |
| Decoration         | Ribbons and paper objects in a bounded scene                                                            | No ribbons behind editable text, tables, or task rows                                                                              |
| Motion             | Finite character gestures; optional slow ribbon drift in a bounded stage with a prominent pause control | State feedback only; Mini Bend eye frames only during pending work, no idle character or environmental loop                        |
| Message            | Invite and explain                                                                                      | State the content, result, next action, or uncertainty                                                                             |

These sizes are starting compositions, not a demand to reserve space for Bend. A screen without Bend can be unmistakably Situate through paper, type, color, and shape.

### Attention budget

- In a work area, the content title and main action must be more visually prominent than a character or illustration.
- Use at most one character per functional panel. A character gallery is a reference exception, not a screen pattern.
- Treat a vivid illustration as one visual unit. Surround it with quiet margins rather than more badges, gradients, stickers, and announcements.
- A daily completion normally changes a checkbox and count. It does not trigger confetti, a full-screen overlay, or a congratulatory message from Bend.
- Never require someone to acknowledge the mascot to continue. Dismissing decoration does not remove information or functionality.

## Warmth without infantilizing

| Do                                                                | Don't                                                        |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Use a sky panel, a small paper fold, and a concise headline       | Add eyes to every note, book, and navigation item            |
| Give Bend a small open smile or thoughtful sideways glance        | Add tears, pleading eyes, blush hearts, or dependency cues   |
| Let the person name a space for their interests                   | Assign a productivity persona, rank, or team role            |
| Mark a meaningful finished result once                            | Reward every click or punish inactivity with streak language |
| Use small asymmetries in art and consistent alignment in controls | Tilt buttons and inputs to make the interface look playful   |
| Leave an unanswered question visible in words                     | Use a worried face as the sole error message                 |

## Personal ownership, not collaboration software

“My space,” “Reading notes,” and “Next up” describe a person's material. A reading-circle example can contain that person's notes about a meeting; it is not a jointly edited space. Do not introduce member avatars, invitations, permission roles, team activity feeds, or seats as brand shorthand.

Describe optional external connections in the terms of the actual service and scope. Do not suggest that every external document is automatically copied into Situate. Avoid organization-wide KPIs, control-room graphics, network-node diagrams, and “command center” language as the default picture of personal organization.

## AI accountability

Use a visible source and a reviewable result where the example depicts AI-assisted work. Explain the action in ordinary terms: “Reading the shortlist,” not “Autonomous orchestration.” Do not claim an external message has been sent when the example only prepares one.

The intended product boundary allows reversible internal organization within a user request and requires approval for external effects. The visual system does not implement or certify that boundary. Bend must not imply continuous surveillance, independent goal-setting, infallibility, or safety approval. A pleased expression says nothing about the correctness of a result.

## Relationship to product goals

| Goal                                                                                                 | Brand implication                                               | What it does not establish                                |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Connected organization](../../../requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0001-connected-organization.md)       | Show meaningful relationships among tasks, notes, and documents | Universal integrations or automatic migration             |
| [Less organizational effort](../../../requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0002-organizational-processes.md) | Make context and prepared results easy to scan                  | A measured time saving                                    |
| [Track commitments](../../../requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0003-track-commitments.md)                 | Keep state legible and avoid decorative distraction             | Guaranteed follow-up or a mandatory organizational method |
| [Decision authority](../../../requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0004-decision-authority.md)               | Give actions specific labels and uncertainty explicit wording   | Verified approval or reversal safeguards                  |
| [Personal connection](../../../requirements/data/goals/GOAL-0005-personal-connection.md)             | Leave room for individual interests and expressive moments      | Evidence that users feel emotionally connected            |

## Review a new design

Ask whether the page still feels personal without the character, whether the next action is obvious without animation, and whether the illustration can stop moving without losing its meaning. If any answer is no, fix the composition before adding more personality.
