## Origin of the identity

Bend, the Situate wordmark, and the paper-and-ribbon illustrations are original project SVG artwork. The selected identity combines the refined open-arch character with the warm palette, expressive compositions, typography, and work-surface density of Bend in Motion. The reusable renderer, static exports, and SVG scene sources preserve that identity without requiring an image trace or a separate asset library.

Phantom informed the compositional approach: a confident, colorful illustrated world around a much quieter functional surface. No Phantom ghost, logo, code, font, illustration, or other asset is included. This is a record of inspiration, not affiliation, endorsement, or a claim to rights in third-party work.

Mini Bend preserves the `quiet-mark` geometry from the original **Soft precision**, direction 04 of the six early studies: `design/brand-discovery/kinetic/index.html` on `archive/brand-explorations-2026-09-30`, commit `6a00ee9`. The compact source retains that open arch and eye placement, uses the current palette in monochrome, and adds original ASCII frames and restrained eye motion. The archive is an origin record, not a runtime dependency.

The detailed construction recipes and reusable component rules in this handbook are design decisions. They are not user-tested findings, product-performance evidence, or a statement that every example is implemented in the application.

## Asset manifest

The [illustration catalogue](../reference/illustrations.html) is the visual selection point for reusable objects, background elements and compositions. This manifest records their editable sources, origins and intended use. Original project SVGs contain no third-party image or font dependencies.

Scene files are transparent compositions; their field color and any live Bend instance are supplied by the containing page. The character stays separate and comes from its shared renderer. The Listening room's inline artwork and CSS cover motifs belong to that composition example.

The wordmark is vector artwork, not typeset text converted from a third-party font. Interface and display font stacks use whatever fonts are installed on the person's OS. No font binaries are included; naming an installed font in CSS does not grant redistribution rights to that font.

No downloaded photographs, stock illustrations, remote icon sets, or third-party character assets are required by this package. If adding outside material, verify its license and attribution requirements before including it, and record its actual source here. Do not treat the absence of a watermark or the ability to view an asset publicly as permission to reuse it.

## Synthetic content and demonstration scope

The reading lists, notes, book titles, and personal projects in the brand reference, and the albums, artists, tracks, and notes in the Listening room, are synthetic examples. They are not customer records, interview excerpts, or research participants' content. An example about a reading circle represents one person's notes and tasks, not a jointly managed Situate space.

Interactions in the reference simulate behavior locally. They do not run AI, contact services, implement synchronization, establish a plugin security boundary, or prove persistence beyond the demonstration's stated behavior. Any validation records concern the tested reference and source files, not application readiness. See [validation](../validation/README.md).

## Adding or changing an asset

1. Identify whether the source is original project work, a generated export, or third-party material.
2. For original artwork, retain the editable SVG or renderer and record the filename and intended role here.
3. For generated exports, change the canonical source and regenerate rather than maintaining an independent variant.
4. For third-party material, record the exact origin, author where known, applicable license, required notice, and modifications. Do not include it until reuse rights are established.
5. Inspect all text and metadata for private names, paths, account identifiers, or correspondence. Use synthetic content rather than anonymizing a recognizable private story.
6. Add reusable objects, background elements and compositions to the [illustration catalogue](../reference/illustrations.html) and its inventory checks. Keep one entry per source asset; generated layers and identity marks retain their separate roles.
7. Check relative links and references after moving or renaming files. Keep the handbook, source files, exports and catalogue in agreement.
